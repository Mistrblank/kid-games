Gems in your bag turn into coins when you get back to the grass. Deeper gems are worth more: Ruby 10 · Emerald 20 · Sapphire 30 · Diamond 50. Watch out for falling boulders, bats (tap toward a bat to bop it!), and dirt with orange specks — that's lava! A Rock Golem guards the bottom of the cave. Look for cracked stone: secret passages hide its 3 hearts.
